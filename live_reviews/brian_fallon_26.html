Brian Fallon and the Painkillers

The Albert Hall, Manchester 11/11/2026

It's safe to say Brain Fallon likes to keep busy. Since the original hiatus of The Gaslight Anthem back in 2015, he's put out four solo albums (each with an accompanying worldwide tour) and also an album with his reactivated band in 2023, plus three (by my count) tours with them. He's also found the time to collaborate with the Fender Custom Shop on a rather tasty looking Telecaster. His latest solo tour, in support of September's Not Bad For New Jersey, is what brings him to Manchester's spectacular Albert Hall tonight.

Following the low-key, mostly acoustic Local Honey, released just after the pandemic hit in 2020, Not Bad For New Jersey sees Brian plugging his electric guitars back in and unleashing an album full of upbeat cuts that hark back to the most rocking moments from his other solo albums: Painkillers and Sleepwalkers. Tonight's first song, No One Likes You in NYC, is one of these - a barnstorming opener which sets the tone for the rest of the evening. Armed with the aforementioned Telecaster, Fallon manages to make the song sound even rawer and more rocking than the recording version - no mean feat, given that the album isn't exactly over-produced and tame-sounding. The pace lets up slightly next with Lonely For You Only, but this is still a more upbeat and electrified run through of the Local Honey version. The same goes for the two other songs played from that album, in fact. It's something Bruce Springsteen did to great effect with the 1999 reworkings of Nebraska-era songs, and Fallon's efforts are equally successful.




An acoustic guitar does make an appearance for a couple of songs, but this contributes nicely to the pacing of the set, setting up the more "story teller" songs like Red Lights and Etta James. These are no MTV Unplugged-style stripped back tracks though, with the band remaining on stage throughout. If anything, the slightly acoustic songs make the 100% electric songs sound more rocking. This is especially apparent on the transition between Among Other Foolish Things and Better Before (which, amazingly, isn't a constant fixture in this tour's setlists). The latter song gets a huge crowd reaction, and rightly so as it's one of the strongest tracks from Not Bad For New Jersey.

The backing band for the current tour is completely different from when we last caught Brian Fallon solo in 2018 with The Howling Weather. The Painkillers comprise Nick Salisbury on bass, Emily Wolfe on drums and Nathaniel Murphy on guitar. Murphy is a bit of a hometown hero, having grown up in Levenshulme, and Fallon clearly loves playing with him. The pair exchange wide grins throughout the evening, and the musical chemistry is clear in the full on guitar duels added to If Your Prayers Don't Get To Heaven and Rosemary, which both feature significantly extended outros. It's good to see him get a chance to stretch songs out like this, something which rarely happens with The Gaslight Anthem.
The evening ends with what we can consider Brian Fallon's anthem at this point: A Wonderful Life, the first song from his first solo album, Painkillers. It's a triumphant tune that's perfect for ending on a high note. No encore nonsense here, just 21 songs in around 100 minutes. You'd be hard pushed to venture out into the cold mid-November air feeling anything but elated after a night of top class rock music, performed by someone who gets as much enjoyment out of playing his songs in front of people as those people do from witnessing the performance.

Setlist: Nobody Likes You in NYC / Lonely for You Only / Painkillers / Ladykiller / Hard Feelings / The Big Sleep / If Your Prayers Don't Get to Heaven / On Good Terms / You Have Stolen My Heart / Among Other Foolish Things / Better Before / Etta James / Red Lights / Somewhere You Shouldn’t Be / Forget Me Not / Nobody Wins / Not Bad for New Jersey / Honey Magnolia / Smoke / Rosemary / A Wonderful Life
